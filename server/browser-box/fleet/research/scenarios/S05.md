# S05: logged out, or a checkpoint / CAPTCHA / 2FA appears mid-run on a browser-channel task

Role: scenario stress-tester, group 2. Paper walk of the drafted design; nothing is built. DECISIONS.md and the
verification reports win over dossiers.

**Verdict: breaks.** The single-job pause path (detect, pause, notify, human re-auth, resume) is described end to end and
most of its double-effect protection (intent row before the click, `outcome_uncertain` on a repeat, fencing token on
takeover) holds on paper. It breaks on one confirmed charter violation and several order-of-operations holes:

1. **Queued or scheduled work can take a `work` lease on an identity that is `needs_reauth` or `checkpoint`.** The lease SQL
   and the job claim never test identity `state`. CH says "the identity moves to `needs_reauth` and its work stops" and
   "a platform restricts, warns or suspends an identity: stop all activity". Nothing enforces it (S05-1).
2. Detection of the blocker is spread over four places, and the one that the ladder uses (the decider) is **off by default on
   logged-in pages**. No component owns a deterministic mid-run detector, and the order "blocker check, then ladder" is
   not stated. An L3 rung can click "Continue as <name>" (S05-2, S05-3).
3. Reauth, reconcile and resume have no required order, and a read-back run while logged out can look like "absent" (S05-5,
   S05-6).

## The scenario, pinned down

Platform choice matters. No agent browser on x.com, no LinkedIn browser automation (CH red lines). The clean browser-channel
write is a **Facebook Group post** (no API, `channel_policy.browser_allowed` includes it, `browser_hitl: per_action`,
client's written risk acceptance on file; ARCH §4.7, §8.3).

Call: `publish(identity:"acme-facebook", platform:"facebook", kind:"post", target:"group:<id>", approval_id:"ap_77",
channel:"browser", hitl:"agent_clicks", idempotency_key:"k1")`. Procedure `facebook.group_post@3`, released, steps:
`S1 wake/probe`, `S2 navigate to group`, `S3 open composer`, `S4 fill (verbatim)`, `S5 pre-submit check`, `S6 click Post`
(irreversible), `S7 read-back`. Three sub-cases:

- **5a** the session is gone before the click (login wall at S2 or S3);
- **5b** a checkpoint ("confirm it's you") or CAPTCHA appears **after** the click at S6, so the effect may have happened;
- **5c** a scheduled manifest job for the same identity comes due while 5a or 5b is paused.

Assumptions (mine): Answer A of Q-01; Q-07 default (no DM to any model; no logged-in page to a System 1 vendor until
cleared; S1 §3.6 rule 5); Q-13 yes; viewer and notifier destinations are whatever Justin eventually chooses (pending).

## Walkthrough

### 0. State before the fault

Identity `acme-facebook`: `state: awake`, lease held by the agent job (`purpose: work`, token T), `auth_markers.last_result: ok`
from the W12 probe (ARCH §4.1). Approval `ap_77` is `approved`, subject hash = the exact text, target, account, audience.
`publish` returned `{status: running, job_id}` inside the 90 s budget (ARCH §4.0 item 4); Hermes is polling `job` with `wait_s`.

### 1. Detection (the fault)

**What the design says detects it, in order of where it is written:**

1. **Wake probe P7** (SP §7, ARCH W12): deterministic marker check. Runs at wake and after a human login only (SP P7 text:
   "It runs when an identity wakes for work and after a human login"). Does not run mid-run.
2. **The L2 `blocker` question** (ARCH §2.4: "Everything for a step goes in one request (target, blocker, done, risk)";
   S1 §4.B `page_blocker`, `login_wall`, `captcha_present`). Under the **default**, `account_page` calls to a System 1
   vendor are refused until Justin clears a (provider, host) pair, and `dm` is refused outright (S1 §3.6 rule 5). So on a
   logged-in client page the L2 reader does not exist in the default configuration. S1 itself says the deterministic probe
   "runs first" and the decider is only "a second reader".
3. **"The fleet verifier (per-site login probe and page-state check before and after steps)"** (SH §14 F19). Named once, in a
   failure table, with no spec.
4. **Executor post-condition `expects`** (ARCH §3.7 `steps[].expects`; §4.3 L2 "code first, then the decider's `step_done`").
   A failed `expects` after `S2 navigate` is the likeliest first symptom. It does not say *why*.
5. **Browser-Use only:** `register_should_stop_callback` polls "login wall, checkpoint or CAPTCHA detected from deterministic
   signals (URL patterns, known challenge frames, optionally a decider noul)" every step (BU §8 stop conditions). This is the
   only place a deterministic detector list is written, and it covers L4 `task`, not the L1-L3 executor that `run`/`publish` use.

**Walk 5a.** `S2 navigate` (L0) lands on `/login/?next=...`. `expects` (URL prefix, composer landmark) fails.

- The ladder does not know it is a login wall. ARCH §4.2 step 6 lists blockers *after* the step; §4.3 puts `blocker` inside
  the L2 request. With L2 unavailable (default), the miss climbs to **L3**: Stagehand `observe` through the shim, with the login
  page's tree sent to the GLM provider. The model is asked for the step "navigate to the group", sees a login form and a
  "Continue as <client name>" button (Facebook-style remembered-account prompt; platform behaviour I am assuming, not
  sourced). ARCH §4.3 refuses `fill`/`type` into credential fields. It does **not** refuse a **click** on "Continue as",
  "Log in" or "Sign in with ...". Gate G's classification for a *procedure step* uses the step's declared effect, which is
  "navigate: none". So nothing in the written design stops an L3 click that re-authenticates the client with no password. That
  is an agent logging in (CH invariant 4). (S05-2)
- If the ordering is made right (deterministic blocker check first), the executor returns `blocker: login_wall` and stops.

**Walk 5b.** `S6 click Post`: gate G passes (scope `publish`, approval hash match, governor reserve, composer text hash and
account header OK), **intent row committed** (`effects.state = intent`, token T), click dispatched. The page now shows a
checkpoint. `S7 read-back` navigates to the group feed, sees the checkpoint page, finds no matching post. Per ARCH §4.7 step 7:
"Not found -> `uncertain` plus hold". `receipt_error_visible` (S1 §4.C) only pushes toward uncertain.

### 3. Notification (what the owner sees)

C16 Notifier "tells owners about `needs_reauth`, `checkpoint`, an `uncertain` effect past its grace period"; **destinations are
configuration, not chosen** (ARCH C16; MS N-8, AQ-3). So "what the human sees" is undefined. What the documents imply:

- Owner message: unspecified body. It needs: alias and platform; what was seen (login wall vs checkpoint vs CAPTCHA vs "features
  limited"); what the fleet did **not** do; whether an effect may have happened (5b); what the job will do on resume; link
  expiry (15 min, `estimate`, ARCH §4.8). None of that is written.
- Hermes tool result: `{ok:false, status:"needs_human", error:{code:"needs_reauth", kind:"browser_login", ...}}` with "a viewer
  link" (ARCH §5.5 `needs_reauth` row). In 5b the `publish` error could be `outcome_uncertain` **or** `checkpoint`: no precedence
  rule. `identity_status.needs` is a single value (`reauth|approval|reconcile`) and 5b needs two, in order.
- The viewer link therefore reaches the owner by **two paths**: the notifier and the agent (fleet-identity step 4: "Give the link
  only to the owner or operator, in a private message"). ARCH §6.5 requires that the link alone grant nothing; AQ-1 (operator
  authentication) is open. And the optional plugin's `post_tool_call` hook writes evidence for every `mcp__browser_fleet__*`
  result (ARCH H3, H5): a tool result that contains the link is written into the Obsidian vault, which the same skill says
  must never happen. (S05-8)
- Hermes side (SOUL): "Say it first when I get it wrong... A CAPTCHA, a 'confirm it's you' or a lock means I stop, mark the
  identity, and get a person." The agent reports in plain words. `fleet-evidence` lists the wait under **Needs a person** in the
  daily note; the run note is not written until the job ends.

### 4. The agent must not keep going (5c)

While the identity is `needs_reauth`/`checkpoint`, a **scheduler-created** job for another manifest item comes due (ARCH §4.11:
items "run without a Hermes call"). Walk:

1. C5 scheduler creates the job at `not_before`. No public call is made, so ARCH §4.0 item 2 ("check identity state and hold") does
   not apply.
2. Claim (ARCH §3.9): `SELECT ... FROM identities WHERE identity_id = $id FOR UPDATE`, take the oldest queued job. **No test of
   `state`.**
3. Lease `acquire` (ARCH §3.5): `WHERE identity_id = $id AND (lease_holder_ref IS NULL OR lease_expires_at < now()) AND ($purpose
   <> 'work' OR hold IS NULL)`. `state` is not in the predicate. `needs_reauth` and `checkpoint` are states, not hold reasons
   (the hold reasons are `uncertain_effect|upload_failed|proxy_mismatch|persona_mismatch|owner_hold`, §3.2). In 5a there is no
   hold at all, so nothing blocks the lease. In 5b the hold blocks `work` leases, but 5b's hold only exists because an effect
   went `uncertain`; a post-click login wall with no intent row (5a) or a CAPTCHA before any effect leaves no hold.
4. Result: a `work` lease succeeds on an identity the platform just challenged; the run starts steps on the same browser
   (an awake identity is not re-probed; W12 is a wake step). It will hit the same wall again and pause again, or act
   (navigation, reads, and with a manifest item, a post attempt) on an account that has just been flagged.

This contradicts CH's own comparison table ("The identity moves to `needs_reauth` and its work stops; other identities keep
going") and the escalation rule "a platform restricts, warns or suspends an identity (stop all activity on it; no
work-around)".
MS §4.6.1 also promises a "cool down"; ARCH has no cool-down field, state or duration (S05-4). (S05-1, **blocker**)

### 5. Human re-auth (ARCH §4.8; SP §8)

1. Ticket minted by C14: 256-bit random, hashed, single use, bound to `(identity, purpose, lease token)`, 15 min (`estimate`).
2. Wake for reauth if asleep: `purpose=reauth`, no workers, no probe. (ARCH W1 says a plain `wake` on `needs_reauth` is refused,
   so a purpose-specific wake path exists in §4.8 step 3 but not in §4.1.)
3. The operator opens the link and authenticates to the fleet: **mechanism undecided (AQ-1)**. fleetd runs `takeover` (token
   T+1). The node agent kills the agent workers (gap: one RPC, `to measure`). The Guard and forwarder stay up.
4. The viewer broker proxies only `GET /v1/sessions/debug` and the cast WebSocket (ARCH §4.8 step 5; Steel viewer doc: `?interactive=true`
   is what allows remote mouse and keyboard). `clipboardBridge=true` for paste.
5. The human logs in. Verified: typed printable text, popups (ARCH §4.8 step 6). **Untested, M1 G1.5:** Enter, Backspace, Tab,
   arrows, IME, passkeys, native dialogs, HTTP auth, file pickers. Login forms are normally submitted with Enter or a mouse
   click on the button. A challenge adds cases G1.5 does not list: drag and slider puzzles, press-and-hold, touch-only
   prompts, "approve on your phone" waits, an emailed link that must open in this browser, and an SMS code sent to the **client's**
   phone (the operator is Justin's staff; who relays the code is undesigned). (S05-9, S05-10)
6. Done: wait for cookie-quiet (31 s, ARCH §4.9 step 3), run the probe. `ok`: `auth_epoch + 1`, new journal stored, lease released,
   `state: awake`. Failed probe: stays `needs_reauth`. Ticket expiry: state unchanged, a fresh link is sent.

If the identity sat `awake` through the wait, it held a node slot (1.5 + 0.35 GiB planning figure; 7 per node) with no work
(S05-11).

### 6. Resume without double effects

ARCH §4.2 step 8: "A job paused in `needs_human` or `needs_approval` resumes from its last completed step when the cause is
cleared, because steps are idempotent except effects, which the ledger guards." The fleet-identity skill: "A job that paused for
the login resumes by itself."

**5a (no intent row yet).** Resume at S2. Gate G re-runs all checks, including the approval's `expires_at`, the manifest window, and
the governor (reservation made at preflight; whether it survived the wait is not stated). The composer was lost with the logged-out
page; S3 and S4 repeat. Reasonable, **but "last completed step" is wrong for a browser flow**: S4's completion means "the composer
on that page holds the text", which is false after a takeover. Resume must restart from a procedure-declared restart boundary
(e.g. S2), not from "the next step". The human also may have navigated the tab anywhere; nothing resets it.

**5b (intent row exists).** The ledger blocks a repeat: the second attempt at the same `effect_key` finds `uncertain` and returns
`outcome_uncertain` and does nothing (ARCH §3.6 protocol). Good. But:

- The job must not "resume" at S7 or S6. It needs the **reconcile** path: a read-only job (lease `purpose=reconcile`, allowed
  while held; ARCH §3.5) that looks for the post. That job needs a **logged-in session**, so reconcile must run **after** the
  human has finished re-auth. Nothing orders them. The reconciler is described as running "after grace" (ARCH §3.6 diagram;
  `grace_until` has no value). If grace expires while the identity is `checkpoint`, the reconcile job runs against a challenge
  page. The read-back verifier (`dom_readback`) has no precondition that it is logged in or that it can see the feed; a
  logged-out/challenge page yields "not found". With a "safe after negative read-back" pair marked in policy, state goes
  `reconciled_absent`, which "may be retried" (ARCH §3.6): **a blind-looking retry of an effect that may have happened**.
  CH "What nobody does, ever" says never retry an effect whose outcome is uncertain; ARCH §3.6 and CH invariant 5 differ on
  whether "reconciled_absent" is certain. By default no pair is marked safe, so by default the outcome is `human_resolved`;
  the hazard is the first time someone marks a pair safe (S05-6).
- Resume vs reconcile ordering, and what the job is meanwhile (`needs_human` vs `uncertain`) are unspecified (S05-5).
- DM surfaces: if the post-click page is a message request or inbox, `data_class: dm` forbids any model (S1 §3.6 rule 5), so
  `receipt_error_visible` is unavailable and the check is deterministic only; the deterministic read-back for browser actions
  is itself "a hypothesis" (DEC D13, CH invariant 5).

### 7. Where double effects are, and are not, closed

| Route to a duplicate | Closed? |
|---|---|
| Hermes repeats `publish` with the same `idempotency_key` | Closed: returns the first job (ARCH §4.0 item 3) |
| Resume re-clicks Post after the intent row exists | Closed: `outcome_uncertain`, no action (ARCH §3.6) |
| Stale worker acts after takeover | Closed on paper: token check by node agent and executor; in-flight CDP commands are the one-RPC gap (ARCH §3.5, `to measure`); any effect command in flight at takeover must be `uncertain` by definition |
| Reconcile reads a challenge page and calls it "absent", then a retry-eligible pair is retried | **Open** (S05-6) |
| New `idempotency_key` for the same content/target | **Open**: `effect_key = H(identity_id, task_id, effect_kind, target, content_hash)`; `task_id` is defined nowhere else in the corpus (grep) (S05-7) |
| New approval for the same text after `ap_77` was consumed by an `uncertain` effect | **Open**: nothing shows the approver a prior uncertain or committed effect with the same identity, target and hash (S05-7) |
| The human, with the composer still open in the viewer, posts it by hand | **Open**: `channel=human` is recorded only on the `human_clicks` path; a free takeover records nothing, and CH says the fleet does not log what a human types (S05-7) |
| Scheduled job on a challenged identity | **Open** (S05-1) |

### 8. What the owner and profile see at the end

Owner: a message (content unspecified), the viewer, and afterwards the `Daily/` note with the job under **Needs a person**, then
under done. Profile: `job` shows `done` with an effect `committed (reconciled_found)` or a hold awaiting a human. Cost: nothing in
the fleet budget changes meaningfully (the cost is slot-hours of an idle awake browser and operator time). The checkpoint rate per
platform is the metric that matters and has no baseline (MS G6a.4).

### 9. Variants

- **Wake-time fault.** Probe fails at W12: no workers started, state `needs_reauth`/`checkpoint`, lease released, ticket; the
  job fails or waits. Cleaner than mid-run, and fully specified.
- **Re-injecting the journal** (SP §8.1 step 14): once only. If the session was revoked server-side, re-injecting old cookies may
  itself look suspicious; not analysed (S05-12).
- **`task` (L4) on a client identity.** BU §8's stop callback covers it. Same ordering fix needed for L1-L3.
- **Browserbase-homed identity.** `solveCaptchas` defaults on for every session (Browserbase CAPTCHA doc). docs/engines/browserbase.md
  sets `false`, but DEC D11 is silent (CH invariant 4 already says this is a gap to close).

# S03. Inbound DM and comment triage and replies for 50 identities

Stress test, group 1. Read against the design as written on 2026-10-10. **Nothing described here is built.**
No webhook receiver, connector, developer app or approval exists.

**Verdict: breaks.** Three things fail as designed.

1. **Hermes cannot see the messages it is meant to answer.** The architecture has no webhook receiver, no
   inbound store, no conversation or window record, and no tool that returns an inbound message (S03-1).
2. **"System 1 classifies" is switched off by default.** The charter's data rule refuses DM text to any model
   provider by the fleet, System 1 vendors included, and shadow mode counts as sending (S03-3). The same rule
   leaves Hermes' own model reading every DM, so the rule protects less than it appears to (S03-4).
3. **The red line "no message to a person who has not written first" cannot be enforced in code.** `publish`
   takes any target string, and the fleet holds no record of who wrote (S03-2). Only a human reading the
   approval screen stands in the way.

If Justin accepts a path for the data question and the architecture gains an inbound component, the rest of
the flow (approval, window check, API send, read-back receipt) is sound. Gaps are in the table at the end.

## What the walk assumes

- 50 identities. A token carries one tenant (ARCH §3.4), so if they span several clients there is one Hermes
  profile per client. The walk follows one profile.
- Platforms: Facebook Page (Messenger and comments), Instagram professional (DMs and comments), TikTok
  (comments; DMs only if Business Messaging is approved and the account is outside the EEA, Switzerland and the
  UK), X (off by default, Q-05), LinkedIn (a human sends; nothing inbound is automated).
- API channel only. No browser is woken and no lease is needed (ARCH §4.6). Q-13 and the node plane do not
  matter here.
- Defaults hold: Q-07 "DM content is not sent to a model provider by the fleet" (CH invariant 8); Q-05; Q-06
  caps $0.
- Workload for the arithmetic: 20 inbound DMs and 40 comments per identity per day, which is 1,000 DMs and
  2,000 comments a day. **An assumption, not a forecast.**

### Comments, one variant

A Facebook or Instagram comment has no 24 h window. The webhook gives a comment event (also unbuilt). Its
`data_class` is undefined: ARCH and S1M define `public | account_page | dm`, and a comment on the client's own
public post fits none cleanly (S03-3). A public reply is `publish kind:"reply"`. Hiding or removing a spam
comment is the case that matters: the tool's `kind` list has `delete` but no `hide` or `moderate` (ARCH §5.3),
DEC D2 promises "comment moderation" by API, and the charter red line bans "hiding replies" (S03-11).

## Outside the window: what each platform allows and what the design says

All limits are `official-docs`, read 2026-10-09/10 (SC §3, SPV SP-10, V-4, V-5). None was observed on a live
account.

| Platform | Window | After it | What the design says | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Messenger | 24 h after the user's last message (7 days after a click-to-Messenger ad) | `HUMAN_AGENT` tag: 7 days, **only for a genuine human reply**. A private reply to a comment: within 7 days. After that: nothing. One-Time Notification and Sponsored Messages exist and are not used (SC §3.1.3). | "Drafted, approved by a person, and sent on the proper window or tag" (SC §2.3). | Instagram | 24 h (7 days after a click-to-Direct ad) | `human_agent` tag 7 days; private reply to a commenter within 7 days; Requests-folder threads idle 30 days are not returned. | Same. | Same (S03-6, S03-14). |
| TikTok | No 24 h window. For a non-mutual-follow conversation: 10 messages in the first 48 h, unlimited within 48 h of the user's last reply, then at most 3 until the user replies. "Prohibited from initiating a conversation." | Open beta; not in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK; privacy and security reviews. | MS names "TikTok's 48 h rules" for the tracker. | Tracker rules not specified (S03-1). |
| X | None. A DM is allowed when the recipient "requested or clearly indicated an intent" to be contacted. 500 DMs a day per account. | An AI reply bot needs X's prior written approval. The only sanctioned outcome at expiry is: the conversation is marked closed, the pending approval is withdrawn,
the owner is told, and a person answers in the platform's own inbox in their own session, or nobody does. Nothing
is sent outbound. The design never says this in one place (S03-8).

## Load and cost on the assumed workload

Arithmetic only. Every input is an assumption or a cited price.

| Item | Arithmetic | Reading |
|---|---|---|

## What a minimal working path looks like

Not a design. A list of the smallest additions that turn this walk from "breaks" into "handled":

1. An `inbound` component and tables (S03-1) and a `conversation_id` on reply and DM `publish` (S03-2).
2. A read tool `inbox` (S03-1) and a decision on the data path (S03-3, S03-4).
3. Approvals that know the window, the message and the conversation (S03-8, S03-9).
4. A per-conversation hold (S03-10).
5. The platform rules for bots, tags and X written as code-level refusals (S03-5, S03-6, S03-7, S03-11).

## Where DECISIONS looks wrong or silent

- **D2** promises "inbound-DM replies" and "comment moderation" through the API. D17 has no tool that reads an
  inbound message, and no moderation kind (S03-1, S03-11).
- **D12** blocks "an identity with a held/uncertain effect" as a whole. That fits a browser's single session and
  not API effects across platforms (S03-10).
- **D7 and D8** keep "System 1 picks" central. For inbound messages the data rule (CH invariant 8) disables
  System 1 and leaves the reasoning path open (S03-3).

## Not checked

- Webhook payload shapes, signatures, retry behaviour and delivery guarantees of Meta, TikTok and X. The
  research read the messaging policies, not the webhook documents.
- Whether Meta treats a human-approved AI draft as a bot reply. No source answers it (SPV missed issue 8).
- Whether a Hermes cron session can read the profile's `BROWSER_FLEET_TOKEN` (hermes-packaging dossier, open
  question). It decides how triage is scheduled.
- Real message volumes and reply mix for any client.

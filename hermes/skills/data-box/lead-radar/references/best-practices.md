# Lead Radar: best practices

- **Dry-run first.** The estimate is the preflight, and the plan shows it before anything is spent.
- **Keep windows short.** `days` of 7-14 keeps leads fresh, and a High older than 14 days is downgraded anyway.
- **Only enrich High buyers.** Medium and low leads are context, not a contact list. Enrichment on the High shortlist keeps contact spend proportional to real intent.
- **Read the evidence quote.** Every lead carries the post's own words. A lead whose quote does not show a direct ask is not High, whatever the score says.
- **Watch seller leakage.** Job ads and providers advertising themselves are the main false positives. The run report's per-platform counts show where they come from.
- **Name the offer and the buyer precisely.** The classifier judges against `offer` and `buyer_profile`. A vague offer makes every post look relevant.
- **Report cost per High lead.** That is the number that decides whether a radar is worth running again.
